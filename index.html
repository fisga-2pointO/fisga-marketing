Common Questions

Questions people ask
before they decide.

How do I figure out the right price for my product?

Fisga's Pricing Study identifies what your buyers will actually pay, including where demand drops, which segments are most price-sensitive, and your optimal launch price. Runs in under 20 minutes. No focus groups, no guessing.

How do I know if my business idea will work?

Fisga's Feasibility Study answers the go/no-go question before you spend. It models real buyer demand, market size, and channel fit using U.S. federal data, and delivers a directional answer in minutes, not months.

How do I find my ideal customer and build an ICP?

Fisga's Segmentation Study builds your Ideal Customer Profile from federal demographic, spending, and behavioral data. Named buyer segments with purchase triggers, price sensitivity, and channel preferences. No surveys required.

How do I test a product idea or concept before launch?

Fisga's Concept Testing study validates your product idea, campaign concept, or new feature against real buyer segments. It surfaces adoption likelihood, key barriers, and which segments respond strongest, before you spend on development or media.

How do I position my brand and find the right message?

Fisga's Positioning Study tests which claims and messages resonate most with each of your buyer segments. It identifies your strongest differentiator and the language that moves your target buyer, so your creative team briefs from insight, not instinct.

How is Fisga different from traditional market research?

Traditional research takes 6–8 weeks and $30K–$80K. Fisga delivers the same quality in under 20 minutes using federally grounded data. No panel recruitment, no fieldwork, no waiting. Every finding traces to a named U.S. government dataset.
