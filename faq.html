Common Questions

Questions people ask before they decide.

Straight answers about pricing, feasibility, segmentation, positioning and concept testing, and how Fisga answers each one in minutes using U.S. federal data.

Pricing

How do I figure out the right price for my product?

Fisga's Pricing Study identifies what your target buyers will actually pay, including price sensitivity by segment, the exact point where demand drops (the price cliff), and the optimal launch price. It runs in under 20 minutes using federal consumer spending data. No focus groups, no guessing.

How do I know if my pricing is too high?

Fisga identifies the exact price point where buyer demand drops, called the price cliff, across your target segments and geographies. You see where your current price sits relative to market willingness-to-pay, and what conditions would support a higher or lower number.

Feasibility and go-to-market

How do I know if my business idea will work?

Fisga's Feasibility Study answers the go/no-go question before you spend. It models real buyer demand, market size, and channel fit using U.S. federal demographic and economic data, delivering a directional answer in minutes, not months.

How do I validate a go-to-market strategy?

Fisga's Feasibility Study scores your go-to-market options on channel, geography, pricing, and timing against real buyer behavior and market conditions. It tells you which path gives you the best shot before you commit budget to any of them.

How do I choose the right market to enter first?

Fisga scores launch markets across size, growth, competitive intensity, and buyer fit, telling you which geography gives you the highest probability of early success and the clearest path to expansion.

How do I do market research without a big budget?

Fisga delivers market research grounded in U.S. federal data for a fraction of traditional research costs. No panel recruitment, no fieldwork, no 6-week timeline. A complete pricing, feasibility, or segmentation study runs in under 20 minutes.

Customers and segments

How do I identify my ideal customer profile (ICP)?

Fisga's Segmentation Study builds your ICP and buyer segments from federal demographic, spending, and behavioral data. It identifies your highest-intent segments, their purchase triggers, price sensitivity, and channel preferences, without recruiting panels or running surveys.

How do I find my target market?

Fisga maps your target market from U.S. federal consumer data, identifying who is most likely to buy, where they are concentrated, what they spend, and what motivates them. A complete segmentation study runs in under 20 minutes.

What is an ICP and how do I build one?

An Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) defines the buyer most likely to purchase, retain, and advocate for your product. Fisga builds ICPs from federal demographic, spending, and behavioral data, producing named segments with profiles, purchase triggers, and conversion likelihood. No survey required.

Positioning and concept testing

How do I position my brand or product?

Fisga's Positioning Study tests which claims, messages, and brand angles resonate most with each of your buyer segments. It identifies your strongest differentiator and the language that moves your target buyer, grounded in real consumer response data.

How do I know which message will resonate with my customers?

Fisga tests claim resonance across buyer segments, showing which messages land, which fall flat, and why. The output includes a segment-by-segment messaging framework ready to brief your creative team.

How do I test a product concept before launch?

Fisga's Concept Testing study validates your product idea, campaign concept, or new feature against real buyer segments. It surfaces adoption likelihood, purchase barriers, and which segments respond strongest, before a dollar is spent on development or media.

About Fisga

What is Fisga?

Fisga is a market research and decision intelligence platform that helps CPG brands, operators, and founders answer pricing, positioning, feasibility, concept testing, and segmentation questions in minutes, grounded in U.S. federal data. Every insight traces to a named government dataset. Results in under 20 minutes, not weeks.

What is decision intelligence?

Decision intelligence is the practice of using structured data analysis to answer specific business decisions before committing capital, such as what to charge, which market to enter, how to position a brand, or whether a product concept will sell. Fisga is a decision intelligence platform for CPG brands and operators.

How is Fisga different from traditional market research firms?

Traditional market research firms take 6–8 weeks and cost $30K–$80K per study. Fisga delivers the same quality of consumer insight in under 20 minutes using federally grounded data. No panel recruitment, no fieldwork, no waiting. Every finding is traceable to a named U.S. government dataset.
