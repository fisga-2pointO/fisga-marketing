1. Scope and Roles

This Policy covers information we process when you visit fisga.co, create an account, or use the Service.

For account, billing, and website data, we act as the party that determines how the data is used (a "controller"). For the documents, briefs, and other content a customer uploads to run studies ("Customer Content"), we act as a "service provider" or "processor" on behalf of the customer, and we handle that content according to the customer's instructions and our Terms of Service and Data Use and Processing page. If you are an individual whose information appears in Customer Content, please direct privacy requests to the customer that controls that data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information such as your name, business email address, organization, role, and password.
  • Customer Content that you upload or submit, including documents, briefs, study parameters, and prompts. Customer Content may contain personal information if you choose to include it.
  • Communications you send us, such as support requests and emails.
  • Billing details. Payments are processed by our payment processor. We receive limited billing information such as your plan, transaction status, and the last four digits of your card. We do not store full payment card numbers.

2.2 Information we collect automatically

  • Usage and telemetry data about how you interact with the Service, including requests to our chat and study features, timestamps, feature usage, credits consumed, and prompt and response metadata captured through our model and observability tooling.
  • Device and log data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and diagnostic information.
  • Cookies and similar technologies as described in Section 7.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide, operate, secure, and maintain the Service and generate the Outputs you request;
  • create and manage your account and authenticate you;
  • process payments and manage subscriptions;
  • monitor, debug, and improve the quality, reliability, and performance of the Service, including through telemetry on requests;
  • communicate with you about the Service, including support, security, and administrative messages;
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

4. AI Processing of Your Content

To generate Outputs, we send your inputs and relevant Customer Content to third-party artificial intelligence providers through their application programming interfaces (APIs). We use these providers under terms that permit them to process your content only to provide the service to us, and that do not permit them to use your content to train their models. We also use an observability and prompt-management tool to log requests and responses so we can operate, debug, and improve the Service. We do not use your Customer Content to train our own or third parties' foundation models. The AI providers and other subprocessors we use are listed in Section 6 and in our Data Use and Processing page.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only as follows:

  • Subprocessors. With the service providers listed in Section 6, who process data on our behalf to deliver the Service, under contracts that require them to protect it.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Micstura, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.
  • With your direction. When you ask us to share information or make it available to others through the Service.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary to operate the Service (for example, to keep you signed in) and, where applicable, to understand usage and improve the Service. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling some may affect functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. We retain Customer Content until you delete it or your account is closed, after which it is deleted from active systems and removed from backups within a limited period, except where we must retain it to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. Usage and log data are retained for a limited period for security, debugging, and analytics.

9. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information, including encryption in transit, access controls and role-based database security, and restricted access to production systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Details are on our Data Use and Processing page.

10. Data Location

We and our subprocessors store and process information in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your location.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your role and location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@micstura.com. We will respond as required by applicable law. If your information appears in a customer's Customer Content, we will refer your request to that customer.

U.S. state privacy rights. Some U.S. states give residents rights regarding their personal information, including rights to access, delete, correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, email privacy@micstura.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

12. Children's Privacy

The Service is for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and provide reasonable notice. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.
